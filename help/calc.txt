# BIG CALCULATOR    (type: calc)

A calculator with giant numbers and a fun fact for every answer.
  0 to 9       numbers (each one plays a note!)
  + - x /      plus, minus, times, divide ( and ) work too
  ENTER        works it out
  BACKSPACE    fixes a mistake
  C            clears everything
  Esc          goes back to the menu

Things to try: 12x12   1/3   2x2x2x2x2x2x2x2   111111111x111111111
What happens if you divide by zero? Try it!
