# ROBOT PET    (type: pet)

A little robot lives in the computer. Take care of it!
  1            feed it electricity
  2            play catch
  3            dance
  4            tickle
  5            tell a joke
  6            sleep or wake up
  7            clean up the mess (dust bunnies, screws, oil drips)
  8            oil (if it goes rusty, it needs 2 squirts!)
  Esc          say goodbye (it will wait for you)

Type a word and press ENTER to talk to your robot.
It knows lots of secret words. Can you find them all?
It gets hungry while you're away, and sleepy at night.
If you leave a big mess, it goes rusty and can't dance or play.
