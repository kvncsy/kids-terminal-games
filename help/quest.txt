# THE QUEST FOR THE GOLDEN CROWN    (type: quest)

Explore the land, find the four keys, and open the castle
doors to reach the Golden Crown!
  arrow keys   walk
  SPACE        zap (you need ammo)
  M            the menu: keep going, start over, or pick any level
  Esc          saves your quest and goes back to the menu

Walk into robots to talk to them. They give good clues!
Gems are treasure. Lions are grumpy: a bump costs a heart.
Pushing a boulder moves it. Push one into water to make a bridge!
Weak walls break if you zap them. Zaps fly over water too.
Some places are dark. Find the lamp to see more.
Ghosts say BOO and send you back. Zap them!

# STARTING OVER
  quest reset  starts a brand new quest
  quest check  looks for mistakes in the board files

# MAKE YOUR OWN WORLD
Every place in the quest is a text file in the quest_boards folder.
Ask a grown-up to help you change one or make a new one!
