# PIXEL PAINTER    (type: paint)

Paint pictures one square at a time.
  arrows       move the brush
  SPACE        paint a square
  ENTER        pen down (moving paints) or pen up
  1 to 8       colors
  9            rainbow brush
  0            eraser
  F            fill a whole area
  M            mirror: both sides paint the same
  U            undo (BACKSPACE works too)
  S            save your picture
  O            open your saved pictures
  C            clear (press it twice)
  Esc          goes back to the menu

There are 4 SECRET TOOLS hidden on other letters. Can you find them?
Your pictures are saved in the My Pictures folder.

# STARTING OVER
  paint reset  hides the secret tools again (your pictures stay)
