# SKI HILL    (type: ski)

Ski down the snowy mountain!
  LEFT / RIGHT turn (press again to turn more)
  DOWN         point straight down the hill: fastest!
  UP           stop
  SPACE        jump over things
  Esc          goes back to the menu

Get the stars. Ski between the red and blue flags for 3 stars.
Ramps make you fly! Trees and rocks go BONK.
After 400 meters the snow monster wakes up. RUN!
