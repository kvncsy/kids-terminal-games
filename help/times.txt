# TIMES TABLES    (type: times)

Big flash cards! Type the answer and press ENTER.
  1  WARM-UP       1 to 10 times 1 to 10
  2  BIG NUMBERS   11 to 20 times 1 to 10
  3  MEGA          11 to 20 times 11 to 20
  4  MIX           a bit of everything
  5  LIGHTNING     60 seconds! Beat your record
  6  MY CHART      see every fact you know, in color
  H            a hint that breaks a hard one into easy pieces
  BACKSPACE    fixes a typo
  M            picks another times tables game
  Esc          goes back to the menu

Facts you miss come back more often, so you learn them.
Get a whole times table right and it gets a star on your chart!

  times reset  clears the chart and the lightning record
