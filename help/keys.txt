# HACKER KEYS    (type: keys)

Every key does something!
  A to Z      decrypts a word that starts with that letter
  0 to 9      shows the number and adds points in the corner
  0 0 0       sets your points back to zero
  SPACE       ACCESS GRANTED... or DENIED!
  ENTER       hacks something silly
  Esc         goes back to the menu

# STARTING OVER
  keys reset  sets your points back to zero
