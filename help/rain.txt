# ALPHABET RAIN    (type: rain)

Letters fall from the clouds. Type a letter to pop it!
  A to Z      pop that letter
  0 to 9      pop numbers too (from level 3)
  Esc         goes back to the menu

Missed letters wait in the puddle, so you can still pop them.
Golden letters are worth 5 points!

Pop 25 letters to finish a level. Then you choose:
  ENTER       move on to the next level
  R           play the same level again

Every game of Alphabet Rain starts fresh, so there is nothing to reset.
