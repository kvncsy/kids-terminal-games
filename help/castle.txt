# CASTLE CANNONS    (type: castle)

Aim your cannon and knock down the other castle!
  1 2 3        play the SLEEPY, SILLY or TRICKY computer
  4            two players take turns
  UP / DOWN    tilt the cannon
  LEFT / RIGHT less power / more power
  SPACE        FIRE!
  Esc          back to the start screen

Three hits and a castle falls apart. Blow away the ground
under a castle and it falls down. CRASH!
Too short? More power. Too far? Less power.
Win to get to the next level. Watch out for the wind!

# STARTING OVER
  castle reset back to level 1
